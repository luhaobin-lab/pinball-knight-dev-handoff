Use case: style-transfer / redraw an existing game item.
Image 1 is the object blueprint: preserve its identity as an eye staff, with an off-axis large eye, uneven bone teeth, crooked thorn crown, narrow bone-and-sinew shaft and two ragged hanging strips. Recompose elegantly if needed, but keep the single complete object.
Images 2 and 3 are the mandatory STYLE REFERENCES, cropped from official Pass the Fear game screenshots. Study the actual line-and-shape language in them. Borrow the uneven, expressive ink drawing, deliberately shaped flat color masses, restrained cel shadows and playful unsettling silhouettes. Do not copy the weapons, characters, UI, words, frames, glowing rarity borders or specific symbols from those references.

Completely redraw image 1 as a sophisticated 2D hand-inked dark-fantasy inventory illustration. Ink strokes have controlled but visible human variation in weight, imperfect corners, tapered endings and selective little dry-brush breaks. Use a base color, one strong painted shadow shape and a few small light accents per material. Detail comes from designed shapes and selective contour/hatching, never from hundreds of surface scratches. Make every rib, fold and hook feel individually drawn rather than mechanically repeated.
A large illustrated dirty-ivory eye with an amber/carmine iris is the focus. A cracked ivory orbital cage and crooked tarnished-gold hooks frame it. The lower fleshy lid twists into a few uneven bone fangs, then into a segmented sinewy grip. Keep an eerie asymmetry, a real dark cavity and elegant spaces between the bone arches. Crimson/plum tissue is stylized as hand-painted flat folds, not photoreal exposed flesh. It should be ominous and inventive, not a cute smiling mascot.
Palette: dirty cream, charcoal-black ink, muted gold, deep plum and carmine, a tiny cool accent. Broad readable value masses, calm empty areas between details.
Absolutely avoid photoreal anatomy, PBR/3D-rendered shine, metallic speckling, volumetric glow, glossy bevel filters, elaborate filigree everywhere, random grunge, geometric vector perfection and artificial low-resolution pixel grids. This is the painted source that will be pixelized afterward.
Composition: portrait 2:3, a compact ritual scepter with a substantial head and shorter grip; head occupies about 42% of object height and most of its width. Entire silhouette inside the frame, 8% clean margin. One isolated object on a genuinely transparent background. No text, labels, floor, cast shadow, decoration or UI.
